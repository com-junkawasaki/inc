Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Exact Descent of Ternary Relations along
Incidence Bisimulation Quotients” for *Mathematical Structures in Computer
Science*.

The paper studies an incidence structure whose behavioral equivalence is
generated from boundary observations and whose ternary resonance relation is
specified independently. It proves a necessary-and-sufficient exact-descent
criterion, gives coequalizer and congruence-reflection formulations, and
classifies finite compatibility failures. For a seven-cell simplex selector,
the paper gives a structural, nonenumerative classification of exactly 32
support-minimal obstruction orbits; executable Lean evaluation is retained as
an independent cross-check. The complete theorem package is checked in Lean 4.

The paper does not claim novelty for congruence quotients, coalgebraic
bisimulation, relational quotients, coequalizers, or automorphism-orbit methods
separately. Its contribution is the combined incidence/resonance construction
and the particular finite obstruction invariant. A scoped comparison with
universal algebra, coalgebra, modal logic, relational systems, categorical
incidence hypergraphs, and mechanized prior art accompanies the manuscript.
