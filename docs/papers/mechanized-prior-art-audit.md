# Mechanized prior-art audit

Snapshot date: 2026-07-15

This is a scoped, reproducible search audit, not a systematic review and not
evidence of priority.  A negative keyword result means only that this audit did
not locate an exact match in the stated snapshot.  It must not be paraphrased as
“no prior formalization exists.”

## Target checked

The target is the conjunction formalized in this repository:

1. an equivalence generated by incidence boundary observations;
2. an independently specified, possibly multi-valued ternary resonance
   relation;
3. necessary and sufficient coordinatewise compatibility for exact descent;
4. the quotient as a coequalizer/reflection and its structured universal
   property; and
5. a finite simplex-family obstruction classification, including the
   card-independent 32-orbit result.

Generic quotient, congruence, coequalizer, coalgebra, bisimulation, symmetry
reduction, and finite enumeration results are prior infrastructure, not claims
of novelty here.

## Search protocol

The local dependency was searched at the pinned mathlib revision with

```text
rg -n -i "coalgebra|bisimulation|coequalizer|quotient.*relation|relational.*quotient|obstruction" \
  .lake/packages/mathlib/Mathlib -g '*.lean'
```

Web searches used the official project/library sites and their source
repositories with combinations of `coalgebra`, `bisimulation`, `quotient`,
`coequalizer`, `many sorted`, `finite obstruction`, `forbidden`, `automorphism`,
and `orbit classification`.  Search-engine coverage and terminology variation
make these searches intrinsically incomplete.

## Finite-obstruction search result

The scoped searches located many formalized finite classifications, forbidden-
configuration arguments, automorphism actions, and executable enumerations, but
no exact match for this repository's `3 n^4` compatibility-obstruction family
and its support-minimal 32 automorphism orbits.  The mathematical novelty claim
therefore concerns the particular invariant and classification theorem, not the
method of finite search, orbit reduction, or proof-assistant computation.

## Claim boundary after the audit

Safe wording:

> We formalize a single incidence-specific theorem package combining exact
> descent of an independent ternary relation, its coequalizer/reflection
> universal property, positive and negative models, and a symmetry-reduced
> finite obstruction classification.

Unsafe wording:

- first mechanized quotient coalgebra, bisimulation quotient, coequalizer, or
  many-sorted quotient;
- first formalization of symmetry reduction or finite obstruction methods;
- no prior formalization of the combined target exists;
- priority over terminology or packages not covered by this snapshot.

## Residual external gate

Before a journal submission, repeat the search in MathSciNet, zbMATH, Scopus or
Web of Science where available, inspect citations to the closest papers, and
obtain review from a coalgebra/incidence specialist.  Those external coverage
and judgment tasks cannot be certified by the Lean development itself.
