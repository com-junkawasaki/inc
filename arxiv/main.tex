\begin{abstract}
We study when an independently supplied, possibly multi-valued ternary relation
on an incidence structure survives behavioral identification.  Exact descent to
the incidence-bisimulation quotient is equivalent to coordinatewise
representative independence; the descended relation is unique, and the
quotient has both a kernel-pair coequalizer universal property and an abstract
congruence-reflection formulation.  For a finite carrier of size $n$, failure
is witnessed by one of three coordinate-tagged four-variable certificates,
giving a $3n^4$ search rather than a six-variable search.  Support-minimal
certificates carry an action of the resonance-bisimulation automorphism group.
For a seven-cell simplex selector we prove, without relying on exhaustive
cardinality computation, that there are exactly $32$ minimal obstruction
orbits, partitioned into five structural families of sizes $8,4,4,12,4$.
Positive examples include dimension-graded simplicial families and a small
structural reaction-network model; a natural-number coproduct supplies an
explicit failure.  The theorem package, categorical constructions, structural
classification, and executable cross-checks are machine checked in Lean~4.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Quotienting a structured object is routine only after every relevant operation
and relation has been shown independent of representatives.  This familiar
principle becomes nontrivial when the equivalence is generated behaviorally by
one part of a structure while another relation is supplied independently.  Our
case has two layers: signed boundary data determines an incidence bisimulation,
whereas a ternary relation $\Res(x,y,z)$ records a potentially multi-valued
``resonance'' mode.  Boundary-indistinguishable cells need not be
interchangeable in all three resonance coordinates.

The central question is therefore precise:
\begin{quote}
When is there a ternary relation on the incidence-bisimulation quotient whose
truth on quotient classes is exactly the original truth on every choice of
representatives?
\end{quote}
The answer is the expected congruence condition, but its incidence-specific
consequences are useful.  It yields a categorical quotient theorem, explicit
positive and negative models, and a finite theory of compatibility
obstructions.  The latter decomposes a global six-representative failure into
three local four-variable failures and supports classification modulo the
automorphisms that preserve both incidence bisimulation and resonance.

Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item an exact-descent iff theorem, uniqueness, universal factorization,
kernel-pair coequalizer, reflection, and adjunction;
\item a finite $3n^4$ obstruction criterion and support-minimal orbit theory;
\item a structural, card-independent classification of the $32$ minimal
obstruction orbits in a seven-cell simplex selector;
\item generic graded and simplicial positive families, explicit negative and
non-faithful positive examples, and a structural reaction-network instance;
\item a Lean-to-paper index linking every stated result to checked declarations
and their hypotheses.
\end{enumerate}

We do not claim novelty for congruence quotients, coequalizers, coalgebraic
bisimulation, relational quotients, free algebras, or symmetry reduction in
isolation.  These are established tools
\cite{Rutten2000,Staton2011,Wissmann2020,BurrisSankappanavar,ChajdaLanger2010}.
The contribution is the incidence-derived equivalence, its interaction with an
independent ternary relation, and the resulting checked obstruction package.

\section{Incidence, bisimulation, and exact descent}

The Lean development uses a rich \texttt{Incidence} structure containing a
carrier, typed signed boundary entries with positive multiplicity, a unit and
gluing operation, structural laws, and ternary resonance.  Only the following
reduct is needed for the main quotient theorem.

\begin{definition}[Paper-facing reduct]
An \emph{incidence--resonance system} consists of a set $I$, an equivalence
relation $\Bis$ obtained from the boundary behavior of an incidence structure,
and a ternary relation $\Res\subseteq I^3$.  Write $q:I\to\Quot$ for the quotient
map.
\end{definition}

The full boundary definition matters for the examples and for how $\Bis$ is
generated, but the descent argument depends only on the resulting equivalence.

\begin{definition}[Exact descent]
The relation $\Res$ \emph{descends exactly} if there is a relation
$\overline{\Res}\subseteq(\Quot)^3$ such that, for all $x,y,z\in I$,
\[
 \overline{\Res}(q x,q y,q z)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\Res(x,y,z).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Resonance congruence]
The equivalence $\Bis$ is a \emph{resonance congruence} if
\[
x_1\Bis x_2,\quad y_1\Bis y_2,\quad z_1\Bis z_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\bigl(\Res(x_1,y_1,z_1)\leftrightarrow\Res(x_2,y_2,z_2)\bigr).
\]
\end{definition}

There is always an existential-image relation on quotient classes,
\[
\Res_q(X,Y,Z)\;:\!\Longleftrightarrow\;
\exists x\in X\;\exists y\in Y\;\exists z\in Z,\ \Res(x,y,z).
\]
Without congruence it is a saturation rather than an exact representation.

\begin{theorem}[Exact descent criterion]\label{thm:descent}
$\Res$ descends exactly along $q$ if and only if $\Bis$ is a resonance
congruence.  When these conditions hold, $\Res_q(qx,qy,qz)$ is equivalent to
$\Res(x,y,z)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $\overline{\Res}$ is exact, replace representatives in each coordinate and
use equality of quotient classes.  Conversely, congruence makes the displayed
truth value independent of representatives; equivalently, the existential
image $\Res_q$ reflects as well as preserves $\Res$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Uniqueness]\label{prop:unique}
Any relation on $(\Quot)^3$ exact on representatives equals $\Res_q$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every quotient class has a representative.  Induction on the three quotient
arguments reduces equality to exactness on $(qx,qy,qz)$.
\end{proof}

The criterion also admits a coalgebra-facing decomposition.  Say that resonance
has \emph{mode transport} when replacing bisimilar left and right inputs can
transport every output to some bisimilar resonant output, in both directions.
Say that it is \emph{output saturated} when replacing an output by a bisimilar
one preserves and reflects resonance.

\begin{theorem}[Transport and saturation]\label{thm:transport-saturation}
Exact descent is equivalent to mode transport along bisimilar inputs together
with output saturation under bisimilarity.
\end{theorem}
The forward implication uses full three-coordinate congruence.  Conversely,
transport supplies a suitable output representative after changing the two
inputs, and output saturation first aligns that representative and then changes
the requested output coordinate.

\section{Universal and categorical formulations}

Let a ternary resonance system be a set with a ternary relation, and let a
morphism preserve that relation.  These systems and morphisms form a category
$\mathbf{TRS}$.  A map $f:I\to A$ is bisimulation invariant when
$x\Bis y$ implies $f(x)=f(y)$.

\begin{theorem}[Universal factorization]\label{thm:factor}
Every bisimulation-invariant resonance morphism $f:I\to A$ factors uniquely as
$I\xrightarrow{q}\Quot\xrightarrow{\bar f}A$.  If
\cref{thm:descent} holds, $\bar f$ is a resonance morphism from
$(\Quot,\Res_q)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Define $\bar f([x])=f(x)$.  Invariance gives well-definedness, quotient
surjectivity gives uniqueness, and the existential definition of $\Res_q$
reduces relation preservation to that of $f$.
\end{proof}

Form the kernel-pair carrier
$K=\{(x,y)\in I^2\mid x\Bis y\}$, equipped componentwise with resonance, and
let $p_1,p_2:K\rightrightarrows I$ be the projections.

\begin{theorem}[Kernel-pair coequalizer]\label{thm:coeq}
Under resonance congruence, the quotient projection is a coequalizer of
$p_1,p_2$ in $\mathbf{TRS}$.  The Lean category-theory interface packages the
quotient cocone as a \texttt{Cofork} carrying an \texttt{IsColimit}; the induced
hom-set equivalence is natural under postcomposition.
\end{theorem}

The same theorem is not specific to incidence after the congruence has been
identified.  Let $\mathbf{CTRS}$ be ternary systems bundled with a setoid that
is a relation congruence.  Quotienting defines
$Q:\mathbf{CTRS}\to\mathbf{TRS}$, while equipping a system with equality defines
$E:\mathbf{TRS}\to\mathbf{CTRS}$.

\begin{theorem}[Congruence reflection]\label{thm:adjunction}
There is an adjunction $Q\dashv E$.  Its objectwise unit is the quotient
projection and its hom-set equivalence is the factorization of
\cref{thm:factor}.  Every resonance-congruent incidence-bisimulation quotient is
an instance of this reflection.
\end{theorem}

These categorical results state the universal status of the quotient; they are
not presented as a new general theorem about congruences.

\section{Finite compatibility obstructions}

Negating resonance congruence yields six representatives.  It is more useful to
change one coordinate at a time.

\begin{definition}[Local obstruction]
A local obstruction consists of a tag $c\in\{L,R,O\}$, a bisimilar but distinct
pair $a\Bis a'$, and two fixed entries $u,v$, such that replacing $a$ by $a'$ in
coordinate $c$ changes the truth of $\Res$.  For example, a left obstruction
satisfies
\[
\Res(a,u,v)\not\leftrightarrow\Res(a',u,v).
\]
The right and output cases are analogous.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Coordinatewise classification]\label{thm:coordinate}
Resonance congruence is equivalent to invariance separately in the left, right,
and output coordinates.  Hence exact descent fails if and only if a local
obstruction exists.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Global congruence immediately implies each one-coordinate condition.  For the
converse, move from $(x_1,y_1,z_1)$ to $(x_2,y_2,z_2)$ through the three
intermediate triples, changing one coordinate at a time.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite search]\label{cor:3n4}
For a finite carrier of size $n$ with decidable equivalence and resonance,
exact descent is equivalent to emptiness of an executable obstruction table
with three tagged searches over four carrier entries.  The raw search space has
size $3n^4$.
\end{corollary}

Swapping $a$ and $a'$ is a fixed-point-free involution on local obstructions, so
every swap orbit has two elements.  More structure is obtained from
automorphisms preserving both $\Bis$ and $\Res$.

\begin{definition}[Support-minimal obstruction]
The support of a local obstruction is the set of carrier elements appearing in
its representative pair and fixed entries.  An obstruction is support-minimal
when no obstruction has strictly smaller support cardinality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Minimal orbit criterion]\label{thm:minimal-orbit}
Every failed finite exact descent has a support-minimal local obstruction.
Resonance-bisimulation automorphisms preserve obstruction status, support size,
and minimality.  Exact descent is equivalent to absence of an orbit containing
a support-minimal obstruction.  For finite decidable systems, these orbits form
an executable table.
\end{theorem}

For graph-functional resonance, $\Res(x,y,z)$ is the graph of a selector
$s(x,y)=z$.  Two generic mechanisms generate obstructions: selector disagreement
on bisimilar inputs, and a selected output class containing more than one
element.  Thus exact descent forces selector invariance and singleton selected
output classes under the hypotheses formalized in the development.

\section{The seven-cell simplex classification}

The principal finite example has cells
\[
V=\{v_0,v_1,v_2\},\qquad
E=\{e_{01},e_{02},e_{12}\},\qquad
F=\{f_{012}\}.
\]
Boundary behavior identifies exactly cells of the same shape: the three
vertices form one class, the three edges a second, and the face a singleton.
An ordinary simplex selector supplies graph-functional resonance and fails
exact descent.

\begin{theorem}[Sharp support]\label{thm:support-two}
Every local obstruction has support size at least two.  The simplex selector
has support-minimal obstructions of size two, and every support-minimal simplex
obstruction has support exactly two.  Its representative pair has vertex or
edge shape, never face shape, and both fixed entries belong to that pair.
\end{theorem}

The automorphism group of the simplex resonance/bisimulation structure has
$12$ elements.  Pair transitivity normalizes each ordered distinct edge pair to
$(e_{01},e_{02})$ and gives the corresponding vertex normalizations.  Four
fixed-slot patterns encode the two fixed entries drawn from a representative
pair.  Combining coordinate, shape, distinguished-$v_0$ position, and slot
equality yields eight structural parameter summands.

\begin{theorem}[Structural orbit classification]\label{thm:32}
The support-minimal simplex obstruction orbits are in explicit bijection with a
$32$-element structural parameter type.  They split into five
coordinate-and-shape families with orbit counts
\[
8,\quad4,\quad4,\quad12,\quad4,
\qquad 8+4+4+12+4=32.
\]
The parameter map is injective by an orbit-constant normal-form signature and
surjective by explicit automorphism normalization.  Consequently the count
$32$ does not depend on native enumeration.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof architecture]
Each parameter constructs an obstruction whose fixed slots lie in its distinct
representative pair; a general two-support lemma proves minimality.  The
normal-form signature separates parameter values, proving injectivity on
orbits.  Conversely, \cref{thm:support-two} restricts an arbitrary minimal
obstruction to a vertex or edge pair and four slot patterns.  Pair transitivity
and coordinate-specific normalization carry it to a canonical parameter,
proving surjectivity.  The displayed cardinal arithmetic then gives $32$.
\end{proof}

Lean also evaluates the complete finite tables, independently obtaining $12$
automorphisms and $32$ minimal orbits.  This computation is a cross-check, not
the proof of \cref{thm:32}.

\section{Positive and negative models}

\subsection{Explicit failure and success}

A coproduct of two natural-number incidence systems is an explicit negative
model: boundary behavior identifies representatives that its resonance
distinguishes, so no exact quotient relation exists.

The saturated simplex relation, by contrast, is closed across the three shape
classes.  Its seven cells collapse non-faithfully to vertex, edge, and face,
and resonance descends exactly.  A non-saturated geometric variant declares an
output mode when its shape equals either input shape:
\[
\overline{\Res}(\ell,r,o)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(o=\ell)\lor(o=r).
\]
Vertex--face interaction therefore has two output modes and is genuinely
multi-valued.

\subsection{Graded representation theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Class-mode representation]\label{thm:graded}
Let $g:I\twoheadrightarrow G$ be a surjective classifier whose kernel is
incidence bisimulation.  Suppose the class-mode selector always chooses one of
the two input classes and is compatible with $g$.  Then resonance descends
exactly and the quotient relation is represented by
\[
\overline{\Res}(g x,g y,g z)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(g z=g x)\lor(g z=g y).
\]
Grade-preserving maps induce resonance morphisms and respect identity and
composition.
\end{theorem}

A bounded graded-boundary profile gives sufficient boundary hypotheses for
$g(x)=g(y)$ iff $x\Bis y$: positive-dimensional boundaries lower grade by one,
are nonempty, and equal-grade cells have matching boundary profiles.  This
reconstructs the triangle grading and applies to an infinite node/edge path.

\begin{corollary}[Simplicial families]
Every downward-closed decidable family of nonempty finite vertex sets, with a
chosen unit cell, carries the codimension-one incidence used in the Lean
development.  Two cells are bisimilar exactly when their dimensions agree, and
the represented dimensions give an exact class-mode quotient as in
\cref{thm:graded}.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Structural reaction network}

Signed reactant/product boundaries encode the reversible pair $A\rightleftarrows
B$.  A separate ternary relation records molecular modes exposed by interacting
reaction modes.

\begin{proposition}[Reaction-network instance]
The model's bisimulation classes are exactly ``molecule'' and ``reaction''.
Resonance descends exactly to these two classes, while the source model is
non-faithful and genuinely multi-valued.
\end{proposition}

This is a structural Petri-net-style toy, not a kinetic semantics: it contains
no rates, concentrations, firing semantics, mass-action dynamics,
thermodynamics, or empirical claim.  Established compositional reaction-network
semantics are substantially richer \cite{BaezPollard2017,CategoriesOfNets}.

\section{Presentations, logic, and boundary encoding}

The formal development contains three supporting extensions.

First, unit-normal binary trees form a free empty-boundary incidence.  Adding
irreflexive generator-boundary lists and ternary relation generators gives a
presented free incidence.  Every compatible generator interpretation extends
uniquely to a morphism preserving unit, glue, boundary membership, and
resonance.  Term equations generate the least glue congruence; the quotient is
an incidence exactly when boundary data is representative independent and no
endpoint collapses to its source, and resonance descends exactly when the
generated equation relation is a resonance congruence.  These three iff
conditions form one quotient-admissibility certificate.  This is an objectwise
universal property; we do not claim a proved adjunction between categories of
presentations and incidence algebras.

Second, a propositional observation language generated by one ternary atom,
truth, falsity, conjunction, disjunction, and implication satisfies:
\begin{theorem}[Logical necessity]
Exact descent is equivalent to invariance of every formula under pointwise
bisimilar valuations.  If descent fails, the atomic resonance formula already
separates two such valuations.
\end{theorem}
The translation into the repository's conservative \texttt{IncProof.Formula}
syntax commutes with its forgetful retraction and preserves semantics for
agreeing physical interpretations.  No quotient rule for the
\texttt{IncProof.Derives} judgment is claimed.

Third, every boundary occurrence yields a three-sorted incidence hypergraph
\[
\mathsf{IncidencePoint}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Vertex},
\qquad
\mathsf{IncidencePoint}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Edge},
\]
where endpoint cells are vertices and boundary-source cells are edges.
Structured incidence morphisms induce commuting maps and preserve identity and
composition.  This encoding is deliberately forgetful: roles, signs,
multiplicities, glue, guards, types, and resonance are not recoverable.  It is
not an equivalence or faithful representation of the full theory.  Categorical
incidence hypergraphs already form an established theory
\cite{GrillietteRusnak2018}.

\section{Related work and claim boundary}

Congruence quotients and free algebras are standard universal algebra
\cite{BurrisSankappanavar}.  Coalgebra supplies behavioral equivalence,
bisimulation, minimization, and quotient reasoning
\cite{Rutten2000,Staton2011,Wissmann2020,DorschEtAl2017}.  Bisimulation
invariance and expressive modal languages are also established
\cite{BakhtiariHansen2017,BakhtiariEtAl2019}; Mari\'c studies bisimulation
quotients directly in inquisitive modal logic \cite{Maric2025}.  Quotients and
strong homomorphisms of relational systems precede the present work
\cite{Novotny1997,ChajdaLanger2010}, and many-sorted quotient algebra has been
mechanized in Mizar \cite{Korolkiewicz1996}.  Isabelle's bounded-natural-functor
development includes bisimulation and quotient-coalgebra infrastructure
\cite{BNFOperations}.

Accordingly, the exact-descent criterion is an instance of standard relational
compatibility once the incidence equivalence is fixed.  Our novelty claim is
limited to the combined incidence/resonance theorem package and its particular
finite obstruction invariant.  We make no priority or formalization-first
claim.  A scoped public and mechanized prior-art audit accompanies the source;
subscription-database citation traversal and external specialist review remain
requirements before asserting a stronger journal-level novelty position.

\section{Conclusion}

An incidence quotient preserves an independent ternary relation exactly when
incidence bisimulation is a congruence for that relation.  Beyond this standard
quotient mechanism, coordinate decomposition turns failure into a finite
$3n^4$ obstruction problem, and automorphism normalization exposes a concrete
structural classification: $32$ minimal orbits for the seven-cell simplex
selector.  The categorical, finite, logical, simplicial, and reaction-network
instances are checked in one Lean development, with broad foundational and
applied claims kept outside scope.
