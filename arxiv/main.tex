\begin{abstract}
We study when an independently supplied, possibly multi-valued ternary relation
on an incidence structure survives behavioral identification.  Exact descent to
the incidence-bisimulation quotient is equivalent to coordinatewise
representative independence; the descended relation is unique, and the
quotient has both a kernel-pair coequalizer universal property and an abstract
congruence-reflection formulation.  For a finite carrier of size $n$, failure
is witnessed by one of three coordinate-tagged four-variable certificates,
giving a $3n^4$ search rather than a six-variable search.  Support-minimal
certificates carry an action of the resonance-bisimulation automorphism group.
For a seven-cell simplex selector we prove, without relying on exhaustive
cardinality computation, that there are exactly $32$ minimal obstruction
orbits, partitioned into five structural families of sizes $8,4,4,12,4$.
Positive examples include dimension-graded simplicial families and a small
structural reaction-network model; a natural-number coproduct supplies an
explicit failure.  The theorem package, categorical constructions, structural
classification, and executable cross-checks are machine checked in Lean~4.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Quotienting a structured object is routine only after every relevant operation
and relation has been shown independent of representatives.  This familiar
principle becomes nontrivial when the equivalence is generated behaviorally by
one part of a structure while another relation is supplied independently.  Our
case has two layers: signed boundary data determines an incidence bisimulation,
whereas a ternary relation $\Res(x,y,z)$ records a potentially multi-valued
``resonance'' mode.  Boundary-indistinguishable cells need not be
interchangeable in all three resonance coordinates.

The central question is therefore precise:
\begin{quote}
When is there a ternary relation on the incidence-bisimulation quotient whose
truth on quotient classes is exactly the original truth on every choice of
representatives?
\end{quote}
The answer is the expected congruence condition, but its incidence-specific
consequences are useful.  It yields a categorical quotient theorem, explicit
positive and negative models, and a finite theory of compatibility
obstructions.  The latter decomposes a global six-representative failure into
three local four-variable failures and supports classification modulo the
automorphisms that preserve both incidence bisimulation and resonance.

Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item an exact-descent iff theorem, uniqueness, universal factorization,
kernel-pair coequalizer, reflection, and adjunction;
\item a finite $3n^4$ obstruction criterion and support-minimal orbit theory;
\item a structural, card-independent classification of the $32$ minimal
obstruction orbits in a seven-cell simplex selector;
\item generic graded and simplicial positive families, explicit negative and
non-faithful positive examples, and a structural reaction-network instance;
\item a Lean-to-paper index linking every stated result to checked declarations
and their hypotheses.
\end{enumerate}

We do not claim novelty for congruence quotients, coequalizers, coalgebraic
bisimulation, relational quotients, free algebras, or symmetry reduction in
isolation.  These are established tools
\cite{Rutten2000,Staton2011,Wissmann2020,BurrisSankappanavar,ChajdaLanger2010}.
The contribution is the incidence-derived equivalence, its interaction with an
independent ternary relation, and the resulting checked obstruction package.

\section{Incidence, bisimulation, and exact descent}

The Lean development uses a rich \texttt{Incidence} structure containing a
carrier, typed signed boundary entries with positive multiplicity, a unit and
gluing operation, structural laws, and ternary resonance.  Only the following
reduct is needed for the main quotient theorem.

\begin{definition}[Paper-facing reduct]
An \emph{incidence--resonance system} consists of a set $I$, an equivalence
relation $\Bis$ obtained from the boundary behavior of an incidence structure,
and a ternary relation $\Res\subseteq I^3$.  Write $q:I\to\Quot$ for the quotient
map.
\end{definition}

The full boundary definition matters for the examples and for how $\Bis$ is
generated, but the descent argument depends only on the resulting equivalence.

\begin{definition}[Exact descent]
The relation $\Res$ \emph{descends exactly} if there is a relation
$\overline{\Res}\subseteq(\Quot)^3$ such that, for all $x,y,z\in I$,
\[
 \overline{\Res}(q x,q y,q z)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\Res(x,y,z).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Resonance congruence]
The equivalence $\Bis$ is a \emph{resonance congruence} if
\[
x_1\Bis x_2,\quad y_1\Bis y_2,\quad z_1\Bis z_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\bigl(\Res(x_1,y_1,z_1)\leftrightarrow\Res(x_2,y_2,z_2)\bigr).
\]
\end{definition}

There is always an existential-image relation on quotient classes,
\[
\Res_q(X,Y,Z)\;:\!\Longleftrightarrow\;
\exists x\in X\;\exists y\in Y\;\exists z\in Z,\ \Res(x,y,z).
\]
Without congruence it is a saturation rather than an exact representation.

\begin{theorem}[Exact descent criterion]\label{thm:descent}
$\Res$ descends exactly along $q$ if and only if $\Bis$ is a resonance
congruence.  When these conditions hold, $\Res_q(qx,qy,qz)$ is equivalent to
$\Res(x,y,z)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $\overline{\Res}$ is exact, replace representatives in each coordinate and
use equality of quotient classes.  Conversely, congruence makes the displayed
truth value independent of representatives; equivalently, the existential
image $\Res_q$ reflects as well as preserves $\Res$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Uniqueness]\label{prop:unique}
Any relation on $(\Quot)^3$ exact on representatives equals $\Res_q$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every quotient class has a representative.  Induction on the three quotient
arguments reduces equality to exactness on $(qx,qy,qz)$.
\end{proof}

The criterion also admits a coalgebra-facing decomposition.  Say that resonance
has \emph{mode transport} when replacing bisimilar left and right inputs can
transport every output to some bisimilar resonant output, in both directions.
Say that it is \emph{output saturated} when replacing an output by a bisimilar
one preserves and reflects resonance.

\begin{theorem}[Transport and saturation]\label{thm:transport-saturation}
Exact descent is equivalent to mode transport along bisimilar inputs together
with output saturation under bisimilarity.
\end{theorem}
The forward implication uses full three-coordinate congruence.  Conversely,
transport supplies a suitable output representative after changing the two
inputs, and output saturation first aligns that representative and then changes
the requested output coordinate.

\section{Universal and categorical formulations}

Let a ternary resonance system be a set with a ternary relation, and let a
morphism preserve that relation.  These systems and morphisms form a category
$\mathbf{TRS}$.  A map $f:I\to A$ is bisimulation invariant when
$x\Bis y$ implies $f(x)=f(y)$.

\begin{theorem}[Universal factorization]\label{thm:factor}
Every bisimulation-invariant resonance morphism $f:I\to A$ factors uniquely as
$I\xrightarrow{q}\Quot\xrightarrow{\bar f}A$.  If
\cref{thm:descent} holds, $\bar f$ is a resonance morphism from
$(\Quot,\Res_q)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Define $\bar f([x])=f(x)$.  Invariance gives well-definedness, quotient
surjectivity gives uniqueness, and the existential definition of $\Res_q$
reduces relation preservation to that of $f$.
\end{proof}

Form the kernel-pair carrier
$K=\{(x,y)\in I^2\mid x\Bis y\}$, equipped componentwise with resonance, and
let $p_1,p_2:K\rightrightarrows I$ be the projections.

\begin{theorem}[Kernel-pair coequalizer]\label{thm:coeq}
Under resonance congruence, the quotient projection is a coequalizer of
$p_1,p_2$ in $\mathbf{TRS}$.  The Lean category-theory interface packages the
quotient cocone as a \texttt{Cofork} carrying an \texttt{IsColimit}; the induced
hom-set equivalence is natural under postcomposition.
\end{theorem}

The same theorem is not specific to incidence after the congruence has been
identified.  Let $\mathbf{CTRS}$ be ternary systems bundled with a setoid that
is a relation congruence.  Quotienting defines
$Q:\mathbf{CTRS}\to\mathbf{TRS}$, while equipping a system with equality defines
$E:\mathbf{TRS}\to\mathbf{CTRS}$.

\begin{theorem}[Congruence reflection]\label{thm:adjunction}
There is an adjunction $Q\dashv E$.  Its objectwise unit is the quotient
projection and its hom-set equivalence is the factorization of
\cref{thm:factor}.  Every resonance-congruent incidence-bisimulation quotient is
an instance of this reflection.
\end{theorem}

These categorical results state the universal status of the quotient; they are
not presented as a new general theorem about congruences.

\section{Finite compatibility obstructions}

Negating resonance congruence yields six representatives.  It is more useful to
change one coordinate at a time.

\begin{definition}[Local obstruction]
A local obstruction consists of a tag $c\in\{L,R,O\}$, a bisimilar but distinct
pair $a\Bis a'$, and two fixed entries $u,v$, such that replacing $a$ by $a'$ in
coordinate $c$ changes the truth of $\Res$.  For example, a left obstruction
satisfies
\[
\Res(a,u,v)\not\leftrightarrow\Res(a',u,v).
\]
The right and output cases are analogous.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Coordinatewise classification]\label{thm:coordinate}
Resonance congruence is equivalent to invariance separately in the left, right,
and output coordinates.  Hence exact descent fails if and only if a local
obstruction exists.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Global congruence immediately implies each one-coordinate condition.  For the
converse, move from $(x_1,y_1,z_1)$ to $(x_2,y_2,z_2)$ through the three
intermediate triples, changing one coordinate at a time.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite search]\label{cor:3n4}
For a finite carrier of size $n$ with decidable equivalence and resonance,
exact descent is equivalent to emptiness of an executable obstruction table
with three tagged searches over four carrier entries.  The raw search space has
size $3n^4$.
\end{corollary}

Swapping $a$ and $a'$ is a fixed-point-free involution on local obstructions, so
every swap orbit has two elements.  More structure is obtained from
automorphisms preserving both $\Bis$ and $\Res$.

\begin{definition}[Support-minimal obstruction]
The support of a local obstruction is the set of carrier elements appearing in
its representative pair and fixed entries.  An obstruction is support-minimal
when no obstruction has strictly smaller support cardinality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Minimal orbit criterion]\label{thm:minimal-orbit}
Every failed finite exact descent has a support-minimal local obstruction.
Resonance-bisimulation automorphisms preserve obstruction status, support size,
and minimality.  Exact descent is equivalent to absence of an orbit containing
a support-minimal obstruction.  For finite decidable systems, these orbits form
an executable table.
\end{theorem}

For graph-functional resonance, $\Res(x,y,z)$ is the graph of a selector
$s(x,y)=z$.  Two generic mechanisms generate obstructions: selector disagreement
on bisimilar inputs, and a selected output class containing more than one
element.  Thus exact descent forces selector invariance and singleton selected
output classes under the hypotheses formalized in the development.

\section{The seven-cell simplex classification}

The principal finite example has cells
\[
V=\{v_0,v_1,v_2\},\qquad
E=\{e_{01},e_{02},e_{12}\},\qquad
F=\{f_{012}\}.
\]
Boundary behavior identifies exactly cells of the same shape: the three
vertices form one class, the three edges a second, and the face a singleton.
An ordinary simplex selector supplies graph-functional resonance and fails
exact descent.

\begin{theorem}[Sharp support]\label{thm:support-two}
Every local obstruction has support size at least two.  The simplex selector
has support-minimal obstructions of size two, and every support-minimal simplex
obstruction has support exactly two.  Its representative pair has vertex or
edge shape, never face shape, and both fixed entries belong to that pair.
\end{theorem}

The automorphism group of the simplex resonance/bisimulation structure has
$12$ elements.  Pair transitivity normalizes each ordered distinct edge pair to
$(e_{01},e_{02})$ and gives the corresponding vertex normalizations.  Four
fixed-slot patterns encode the two fixed entries drawn from a representative
pair.  Combining coordinate, shape, distinguished-$v_0$ position, and slot
equality yields eight structural parameter summands.

\begin{theorem}[Structural orbit classification]\label{thm:32}
The support-minimal simplex obstruction orbits are in explicit bijection with a
$32$-element structural parameter type.  They split into five
coordinate-and-shape families with orbit counts
\[
8,\quad4,\quad4,\quad12,\quad4,
\qquad 8+4+4+12+4=32.
\]
The parameter map is injective by an orbit-constant normal-form signature and
surjective by explicit automorphism normalization.  Consequently the count
$32$ does not depend on native enumeration.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof architecture]
Each parameter constructs an obstruction whose fixed slots lie in its distinct
representative pair; a general two-support lemma proves minimality.  The
normal-form signature separates parameter values, proving injectivity on
orbits.  Conversely, \cref{thm:support-two} restricts an arbitrary minimal
obstruction to a vertex or edge pair and four slot patterns.  Pair transitivity
and coordinate-specific normalization carry it to a canonical parameter,
proving surjectivity.  The displayed cardinal arithmetic then gives $32$.
\end{proof}

Lean also evaluates the complete finite tables, independently obtaining $12$
automorphisms and $32$ minimal orbits.  This computation is a cross-check, not
the proof of \cref{thm:32}.

\section{Positive and negative models}

\subsection{Explicit failure and success}

A coproduct of two natural-number incidence systems is an explicit negative
model: boundary behavior identifies representatives that its resonance
distinguishes, so no exact quotient relation exists.

For a sum carrying the exact representative-control certificate, the
canonical classifier instead factors uniquely through the bisimulation
quotient.  Restricting its codomain to reachable observations produces an
isomorphism of ternary resonance systems.  On the category whose morphisms
preserve both sum resonance and bisimulation, quotient and reachable-image
assignments are functors and these comparison isomorphisms form a natural
isomorphism.  This is a conditional controlled-sum theorem, not unconditional
closure of arbitrary incidence coproducts under quotient descent.

The saturated simplex relation, by contrast, is closed across the three shape
classes.  Its seven cells collapse non-faithfully to vertex, edge, and face,
and resonance descends exactly.  A non-saturated geometric variant declares an
output mode when its shape equals either input shape:
\[
\overline{\Res}(\ell,r,o)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(o=\ell)\lor(o=r).
\]
Vertex--face interaction therefore has two output modes and is genuinely
multi-valued.

\subsection{Graded representation theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Class-mode representation]\label{thm:graded}
Let $g:I\twoheadrightarrow G$ be a surjective classifier whose kernel is
incidence bisimulation.  Suppose the class-mode selector always chooses one of
the two input classes and is compatible with $g$.  Then resonance descends
exactly and the quotient relation is represented by
\[
\overline{\Res}(g x,g y,g z)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(g z=g x)\lor(g z=g y).
\]
Grade-preserving maps induce resonance morphisms and respect identity and
composition.
\end{theorem}

A bounded graded-boundary profile gives sufficient boundary hypotheses for
$g(x)=g(y)$ iff $x\Bis y$: positive-dimensional boundaries lower grade by one,
are nonempty, and equal-grade cells have matching boundary profiles.  This
reconstructs the triangle grading and applies to an infinite node/edge path.

\begin{corollary}[Simplicial families]
Every downward-closed decidable family of nonempty finite vertex sets, with a
chosen unit cell, carries the codimension-one incidence used in the Lean
development.  Two cells are bisimilar exactly when their dimensions agree, and
the represented dimensions give an exact class-mode quotient as in
\cref{thm:graded}.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Structural reaction network}

Signed reactant/product boundaries encode the reversible pair $A\rightleftarrows
B$.  A separate ternary relation records molecular modes exposed by interacting
reaction modes.

\begin{proposition}[Reaction-network instance]
The model's bisimulation classes are exactly ``molecule'' and ``reaction''.
Resonance descends exactly to these two classes, while the source model is
non-faithful and genuinely multi-valued.
\end{proposition}

This is a structural Petri-net-style toy, not a kinetic semantics: it contains
no rates, concentrations, firing semantics, mass-action dynamics,
thermodynamics, or empirical claim.  Established compositional reaction-network
semantics are substantially richer \cite{BaezPollard2017,CategoriesOfNets}.

\section{Presentations, logic, and boundary encoding}

The formal development contains three supporting extensions.

First, unit-normal binary trees form a free empty-boundary incidence.  Adding
irreflexive generator-boundary lists and ternary relation generators gives a
presented free incidence.  Every compatible generator interpretation extends
uniquely to a morphism preserving unit, glue, boundary membership, and
resonance.  Term equations generate the least glue congruence; the quotient is
an incidence exactly when boundary data is representative independent and no
endpoint collapses to its source, and resonance descends exactly when the
generated equation relation is a resonance congruence.  These three iff
conditions form one quotient-admissibility certificate.  This is an objectwise
universal property; we do not claim a proved adjunction between categories of
presentations and incidence algebras.

Second, a propositional observation language generated by one ternary atom,
truth, falsity, conjunction, disjunction, and implication satisfies:
\begin{theorem}[Logical necessity]
Exact descent is equivalent to invariance of every formula under pointwise
bisimilar valuations.  If descent fails, the atomic resonance formula already
separates two such valuations.
\end{theorem}
The translation into the repository's conservative \texttt{IncProof.Formula}
syntax commutes with its forgetful retraction and preserves semantics for
agreeing physical interpretations.  No quotient rule for the
\texttt{IncProof.Derives} judgment is claimed.

Third, every boundary occurrence yields a three-sorted incidence hypergraph
\[
\mathsf{IncidencePoint}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Vertex},
\qquad
\mathsf{IncidencePoint}\longrightarrow\mathsf{Edge},
\]
where endpoint cells are vertices and boundary-source cells are edges.
Structured incidence morphisms induce commuting maps and preserve identity and
composition.  This encoding is deliberately forgetful: roles, signs,
multiplicities, glue, guards, types, and resonance are not recoverable.  It is
not an equivalence or faithful representation of the full theory.  Categorical
incidence hypergraphs already form an established theory
\cite{GrillietteRusnak2018}.

\section{Related work and claim boundary}

Congruence quotients and free algebras are standard universal algebra
\cite{BurrisSankappanavar}.  Coalgebra supplies behavioral equivalence,
bisimulation, minimization, and quotient reasoning
\cite{Rutten2000,Staton2011,Wissmann2020,DorschEtAl2017}.  Bisimulation
invariance and expressive modal languages are also established
\cite{BakhtiariHansen2017,BakhtiariEtAl2019}; Mari\'c studies bisimulation
quotients directly in inquisitive modal logic \cite{Maric2025}.  Quotients and
strong homomorphisms of relational systems precede the present work
\cite{Novotny1997,ChajdaLanger2010}, and many-sorted quotient algebra has been
mechanized in Mizar \cite{Korolkiewicz1996}.  Isabelle's bounded-natural-functor
development includes bisimulation and quotient-coalgebra infrastructure
\cite{BNFOperations}.

Accordingly, the exact-descent criterion is an instance of standard relational
compatibility once the incidence equivalence is fixed.  Our novelty claim is
limited to the combined incidence/resonance theorem package and its particular
finite obstruction invariant.  We make no priority or formalization-first
claim.  A scoped public and mechanized prior-art audit accompanies the source;
subscription-database citation traversal and external specialist review remain
requirements before asserting a stronger journal-level novelty position.

\subsection{Canonical congruence saturation}

The obstruction criterion also yields a canonical positive completion, rather
than merely rejecting noncongruent relations.  Let a ternary relation be
specified directly on the bisimulation quotient and pull it back along the
three quotient projections.  If it contains every output selected by the glue
operation, this pullback is a valid resonance on the unchanged incidence data.
Its bisimulation relation is unchanged and exact descent holds automatically;
the descended relation is exactly the relation originally specified on the
quotient.

Taking the specified relation to be the existential quotient image of the
original resonance defines the \emph{canonical bisimulation-congruence
saturation}.  It contains the original resonance and is contained in every
three-coordinate bisimulation-congruent relation that contains the original.
It fixes precisely the already congruent resonances and applying it twice has
the same effect as applying it once.  Thus it is an extensive, least, and
idempotent completion at the resonance level.  It deliberately leaves the
carrier, boundary, type, and glue unchanged; it is not a construction of a full
incidence object on the quotient carrier.

\paragraph{Resonance-saturation reflection.}
Fix the carrier, boundary, type, and glue data, and order all admissible
resonance extensions pointwise.  The canonical saturation is monotone,
extensive, and idempotent, hence defines a closure operator on this poset.  Its
closed elements are exactly the extensions for which bisimulation is a ternary
congruence.  Saturation and the inclusion of closed extensions form a Galois
insertion, so this is a reflector between the associated thin categories and
the closure is its idempotent monad.  This first statement is fiberwise over
one fixed incidence skeleton.

\paragraph{Global saturation adjunction.}
Fix only the boundary-role type and allow carriers and cell types to vary.
Morphisms preserve the unit, selected glue, boundary occurrences, resonance,
and bisimulation.  These data form a category, and saturation acts
functorially on its carrier-changing morphisms.  The extensive inclusion is a
natural unit and the square of the saturation functor is naturally isomorphic
to saturation itself.  Quotient-congruent objects form a structured
subcategory, and saturation into it is left adjoint to inclusion.  Equivalently,
every morphism into a congruent target factors uniquely through the saturation
unit.  A checked non-surjective map from a one-point to a two-point trivial
incidence witnesses that this category genuinely permits carrier changes.  The
present category fixes the role type and requires only forward
boundary preservation; role-changing or boundary-reflecting morphisms are not
claimed in this weaker category.

\paragraph{Role-changing exact-boundary adjunction.}
A stronger category allows carrier, role, and cell-type maps simultaneously.
Its morphisms preserve the algebraic and behavioral data above and require an
if-and-only-if law for membership of every mapped boundary occurrence.  The
saturation functor, natural unit, idempotence isomorphism, congruent
subcategory, adjunction, and unique factorization all lift to this category.  A
non-surjective map from a one-point incidence with role type
\texttt{GraphRole} to a two-point incidence with role type \texttt{Unit}
witnesses both carrier and role change.  Boundary exactness concerns mapped
occurrences; essential surjectivity onto all target boundary occurrences is
not required in this intermediate category.

\paragraph{Globally boundary-surjective adjunction.}
The strongest morphism class considered here additionally requires every
boundary occurrence at every target cell to have a source cell and source
occurrence mapping to it.  Identity and composition preserve this global
coverage law.  Since saturation changes only resonance, its functor, natural
unit, idempotence isomorphism, congruent subcategory, adjunction, and unique
factorization all restrict to these maps.  The role-changing one-point to
two-point example remains carrier-nonsurjective but covers all target boundary
data.  Thus the theorem asserts essential surjectivity of boundary occurrences,
not surjectivity onto target cells having empty boundary.

\paragraph{Carrier-and-boundary-surjective adjunction.}
Finally, imposing surjectivity on the carrier map as well gives the strongest
morphism category formalized here.  It is closed under identity and
composition, and saturation again has its natural unit, idempotence
isomorphism, congruent-object adjunction, and unique factorization.  A checked
map from a two-point incidence with role type \texttt{GraphRole} onto a
one-point incidence with role type \texttt{Unit} is surjective but
noninjective, showing that this category is not restricted to isomorphisms.

\paragraph{Internal-logic conservativity.}
Saturation is monotone at the level of resonance atoms.  Consequently the
positive language generated by truth, falsity, atoms, conjunction, and
disjunction is preserved from every incidence into its saturation.  The full
language also contains implication and is not monotone in this sense.  Full
preservation and reflection for every formula holds exactly when the source
resonance is already a bisimulation congruence; equivalently, every source
formula is bisimulation invariant.  Every formula is invariant after
saturation.  The ordinary simplex is a strict witness: its
\texttt{v1/face/face} observation atom is false before saturation and true
afterwards.  These theorems transfer through the mechanized
resonance-to-\texttt{IncProof} translation whenever the encoded atomic truth
predicates agree with the source and saturated resonance relations.

\paragraph{Dependent-calculus conservativity.}
The normalized dependent completion for the raw $\Pi/\Sigma/\mathsf{Id}$
calculus bundles structural renaming and substitution preservation with
resonance symmetry, units, type compatibility, relational associativity, and
quotient congruence.  Its congruence field makes the resonance relation a fixed
point of saturation.  Hence the completion transfers to saturation while all
formation and typing renaming/substitution outputs remain definitionally
unchanged.  When paired with the public completed-typing interpreter, every
semantic interpretation and its substitution-coherence theorem are unchanged
as well.  The natural-number completion is a concrete instance.  This is a
conservativity theorem for an existing normalized completion; saturation alone
does not synthesize associativity for an arbitrary resonance.

\paragraph{Associative saturation completion.}
There is also a genuinely generative dependent result.  Saturation preserves
resonance symmetry, units, and type compatibility unconditionally.  If source
modes transport along bisimilar input representatives, source relational
associativity transports to saturation: the transport law aligns the two
representatives of each intermediate mode before applying associativity.
Consequently structural preservation, resonance laws, associativity, and mode
transport suffice to construct a normalized dependent completion; saturation
supplies the missing quotient congruence.  The two-point trivial incidence is a
strict example.  Its bisimulation is total, its functional resonance is
associative and transports modes, but it is not output-saturated.  Saturation
adds the \texttt{true/true/false} mode and remains associative.  No preservation
of associativity is claimed without the mode-transport hypothesis.

\paragraph{Exact quotient-associativity criterion.}
For an arbitrary source relation, associativity can be stated directly for its
existential relation on bisimulation classes, without assuming exact descent.
Saturated resonance is associative if and only if this quotient relation is
associative.  One direction descends a saturated intermediate mode to its
class; the other chooses a representative of the quotient intermediate mode.
Thus mode transport is a sufficient source-level proof principle, not a
necessary condition.  Structural preservation, source resonance laws, and the
exact quotient-associativity condition directly generate a normalized
saturated dependent completion.

\paragraph{Associativity obstruction certificates.}
Failure of quotient associativity has a carrier-level certificate.  A
directional candidate records four values such that one parenthesization has a
two-step saturated resonance chain while the opposite parenthesization has no
intermediate mode.  Such certificates are absent exactly when saturated
resonance is associative, equivalently when the existential quotient relation
is associative.  On a finite carrier all candidates form a finite table and
table emptiness is the exact criterion.  The two-point strict-saturation model
has an empty table.  This first table uses classical decidability; no native
enumeration or automorphism-minimized classification is claimed.

\paragraph{Executable associativity decision.}
Given exact Boolean checkers for source bisimulation and resonance, nested
finite searches compute saturated modes and the two possible two-step chains.
A Boolean directional checker is proved correct for obstruction certificates,
and filtering all candidates produces an executable finite table.  Its
emptiness is equivalent to quotient associativity.  For the two-point strict-
saturation model, table emptiness is evaluated by \texttt{native\_decide}.
Automorphism-orbit and support-minimal compression remain optional
optimizations, not correctness prerequisites.

\paragraph{Ternary interaction representation.}
Every small ternary relational system admits a canonical Incidence
representation after freely adjoining a unit.  The original carrier is the
nonunit sector, and resonance on three original elements holds exactly when
the source ternary relation holds.  Relation-preserving maps extend
functorially, and the resulting functor is faithful.  Restriction back to the
nonunit sector recovers the source system up to a checked isomorphism.  This is
a representation theorem for ternary interaction systems, not yet for
arbitrary categories or dependent type theories; boundary and cell-type data
are intentionally trivial in this first construction.

\paragraph{Small-category composition representation.}
For every small category, take endpoint-tagged arrows as a carrier and declare
a ternary mode precisely when the first two arrows are composable and the
third is their composite.  This relation recognizes ordinary composition
exactly, is single-valued, and satisfies relational associativity.  Every
functor induces a resonance-preserving map, compatibly with identity and
functor composition.  Applying the preceding free-unit construction gives an
Incidence representation and recovers the composition system from its nonunit
sector.  The later recovery and scoped-fullness results strengthen this to
typed categorical data and an exact morphism image. Higher categories are not
covered.

\paragraph{Internal category recovery.}
The same representation also recovers the typed categorical data.  Objects
embed injectively as identity arrows, and the original object type is
equivalent to that image.  For each object pair $(X,Y)$, the hom type
$\mathcal C(X,Y)$ is equivalent to the fiber of represented arrows with source
$X$ and target $Y$.  Under these fiber equivalences, Incidence resonance holds
exactly when the recovered output equals categorical composition.  The bundled
certificate therefore recovers objects, hom types, and composition for every
small category.  It uses the known identity embedding and endpoint
projections; the following layers supply an intrinsic identity predicate and
scoped fullness. Univalence and higher coherence are not claimed.

\paragraph{Intrinsic identity and object recovery.}
The external choice of the identity-arrow image can also be removed.  Define
an arrow to be intrinsically unital when its endpoints agree and it acts as a
left and right unit for every endpoint-compatible arrow under the ternary
composition relation.  This relational predicate holds exactly for the
identity arrow on its source.  Hence the original object type is equivalent to
the subtype of intrinsically unital arrows, and the preceding hom-fiber and
composition recovery gives an intrinsic recovery certificate.  Endpoint
projections remain part of the predicate; the following result supplies
scoped fullness. Univalence and higher coherence are not asserted.

\paragraph{Scoped fullness and exact functor image.}
The morphism-level comparison can also be made exact.  A functorial
category-composition homomorphism carries object and hom actions satisfying
the identity and composition laws, and its resonance map is the induced map
on endpoint-tagged arrows.  Every such homomorphism is induced by a unique
functor.  Conversely, an arbitrary ternary-resonance homomorphism belongs to
the functor image exactly when it admits this structure.  Thus the
representation is faithful and full on precisely characterized admissible
morphisms.  We do not claim unrestricted fullness for bare relation maps,
which need not preserve endpoints or identities.

\paragraph{Pure relational category recovery.}
Endpoint projections can be removed from the recovery specification.  Call an
arrow a pure identity when every defined left or right composite with it is
unchanged.  This predicate uses only the ternary composition relation and is
proved equivalent to being a categorical identity.  The source identity of an
arrow is then the unique pure identity whose left composite is that arrow, and
the target identity is characterized dually.  The original object type is
equivalent to the pure-identity subtype; each hom type is equivalent to the
fiber defined by these two relational endpoint predicates; and composition is
recovered exactly on those fibers.  This is the representation and recovery
direction; the following result supplies an abstract recognition interface.
It is not a univalence result.

\paragraph{Relational category recognition.}
There is also a converse construction for abstract ternary systems.  Assume
functionality, self-composition of pure identities, unique relational source
and target identities, existence of composition exactly when the middle
identity matches, stability of outer endpoints, and relational associativity.  Pure
identities then form the objects of a category; arrows with fixed relational
endpoints form its hom types; and the unique resonant output defines
composition.  The category laws follow from the identity, functionality, and
associativity axioms.  Every small-category composition system satisfies this
interface, every carrier element appears as a typed reconstructed hom, and
resonance on typed homs holds exactly for reconstructed composition.  We do
not claim independence or minimality of these axioms, univalence, or higher
coherence.

\paragraph{Relational category system round trip.}
The reconstruction is exact at the ternary-system level.  Map each original
carrier element to the endpoint-tagged arrow whose objects are its uniquely
selected source and target identities and whose hom is the element itself;
decode by taking the underlying hom value.  These maps are mutually inverse,
including their dependent endpoint fields.  Moreover, original resonance
holds exactly when the three encoded arrows satisfy reconstructed categorical
composition.  They therefore define an isomorphism from the original ternary
system to the composition system of its reconstructed category.  The proof
uses classical choice for the unique endpoints and composite.  It does not
establish an equivalence of categories of all models and morphisms, axiom
minimality, univalence, or higher coherence.

\paragraph{Functorial relational category reconstruction.}
The reconstruction also extends to morphisms.  Bundle recognized ternary
systems with the preceding axioms and take as morphisms the carrier maps that
preserve resonance and pure identities.  These objects and morphisms form a
category.  Each morphism preserves relational source and target identities and
therefore induces a functor between the reconstructed categories.  This
assignment preserves identity and composition exactly.  Moreover, encoding an
element before applying the induced arrow map agrees with first applying the
system morphism and then encoding; decoding satisfies the dual equality.  Thus
the round trip is natural at the morphism level.  The following result packages
this assignment as one functor; a categorical equivalence is not asserted here.

The reconstructed category objects and reconstructed functors assemble into a
single universe-explicit functor from recognized ternary systems to mathlib's
category \texttt{Cat}.  Its object action is definitionally the category of
pure identities and relational endpoint fibers, and its morphism action is the
preceding reconstructed functor.  The identity and composition laws are those
already checked, while the capstone retains encoding naturality.  We do not
The following result constructs the inverse-direction functor; natural
isomorphisms of the resulting composites and a categorical equivalence are not
part of this theorem.

\paragraph{Category recognition inverse-direction functor.}
Conversely, every mathlib \texttt{Cat} object is sent to its endpoint-tagged
ternary composition system equipped with the recognition axioms, and every
functor is sent to its map on tagged arrows.  Functors preserve resonance and
pure identities, and the induced recognized morphisms preserve identity and
composition exactly.  Hence these assignments form one functor from
\texttt{Cat} to recognized ternary systems.  Together with the preceding
reconstruction functor, both directions are now packaged.  The following
result supplies the recognized-system-side natural isomorphism; the Cat-side
one remains to obtain a categorical equivalence.

\paragraph{Recognized-system round-trip natural isomorphism.}
Exact relation transport shows that encoding and decoding preserve pure
identities, not merely resonance.  They therefore define inverse morphisms in
the category of recognized systems at every object.  The morphism naturality
proved above assembles these component isomorphisms into a natural isomorphism
from the identity functor to reconstruction followed by recognition.  This is
one of the two natural isomorphisms required for a categorical equivalence; the
Cat-side round trip is supplied objectwise by the following theorem.

\paragraph{Category round-trip equivalence.}
For every small category, send an object to its pure identity arrow in the
reconstructed category and send a morphism to the corresponding relational
endpoint fiber.  This canonical functor is faithful because tagged arrows
retain their hom value.  It is full because every morphism between canonical
identity objects has the required original endpoints, and it is essentially
surjective because every pure identity is the identity of its recovered source
object.  Consequently every category is equivalent to the category recovered
from its ternary composition system.  This is an objectwise equivalence of
categories; a Cat-valued natural isomorphism and a single global equivalence of
the two model categories are not claimed.  The following theorem supplies the
functor naturality of these objectwise equivalences.

\paragraph{Category round-trip functor naturality.}
For every original functor, first applying the canonical round-trip embedding
and then its reconstructed functor agrees exactly with first applying the
original functor and then embedding.  The two paths are equal as functors, with
separate checked object and dependent-hom equations.  This is the naturality
appropriate to the objectwise equivalences above.  We retain the distinction
between equivalence of categories and isomorphism in the ordinary
1-category \texttt{Cat}: a natural isomorphism in that 1-category would require
strict category isomorphisms as its components.

\paragraph{Relational-category comparison capstone.}
The exact comparison level is bundled in one typed certificate: it contains
both directional functors, the recognized-system natural isomorphism, the
Cat-side objectwise equivalences, their canonical full, faithful, essentially
surjective functors, and the exact naturality squares.  This package is
deliberately not labelled a 1-categorical equivalence of model categories,
because isomorphism in ordinary \texttt{Cat} is stricter than equivalence of
categories.  A native bicategorical pseudonatural-equivalence package is a
separate future strengthening.

\paragraph{Dependent judgment interaction representation.}
Fix a context of the raw dependent $\Pi/\Sigma/\mathsf{Id}$ calculus. Tagged
raw types and terms form a ternary system in which a diagonal type interaction
exists exactly when the type is well formed, and a term/type interaction exists
exactly when the corresponding typing judgment is inhabited. Every
derivation-preserving context renaming induces a resonance homomorphism; these
maps preserve identities and composition exactly. The free-unit Incidence
encoding preserves both judgment relations and recovers the original ternary
system after the adjoined unit is removed. This is a representation theorem
for the checked raw calculus, not a completeness theorem for an internal
dependent language over arbitrary \texttt{ResonanceSpec} models.

\paragraph{General resonance laws.}
A carrier-independent propositional axiom schema expresses resonance symmetry,
both unit laws, and the exclusion of every type-incompatible resonance atom.
An arbitrary valuation satisfies the entire schema exactly when it is a
symmetric, unital, type-compatible ternary relation over the fixed incidence
signature. In particular, the physical resonance valuation satisfies the
schema exactly when the Incidence admits a \texttt{ResonanceSpec}. Proofs from
the possibly infinite schema remain finitary: each natural-deduction derivation
uses a finite context of schema instances. Such derivations are sound in every
law model. This removes finite enumeration from general axiomatization and
soundness; strong Kripke completeness for the infinite schema is not asserted.

\paragraph{General resonance-law Kripke semantics.}
A lawful Kripke model requires its valuation to satisfy symmetry, both unit
laws, and type compatibility at every world. Each law-schema instance is
forced at every such world, including all future worlds quantified by
implication and negation. Consequently every finitary derivation from the full
schema is Kripke-sound without enumerating the carrier. Strong completeness is
proved equivalent to one explicit remaining obligation: every nonderivable
formula must fail at some world of a lawful Kripke model. Thus the unchecked
part is isolated as an infinite-schema canonical-countermodel construction;
the manuscript does not assert that this final obligation is discharged.

\paragraph{Countable-schema completeness.}
For a resonance-atom language equipped with a countable coding, the remaining
countermodel obligation is discharged. Schema-relative derivations admit finite
support and form a deductively closed theory. A relative avoidance chain then
constructs a prime theory containing every law instance while excluding a
chosen nonderivable formula. The upper cone above this theory is a lawful
canonical Kripke model: restricting to the cone preserves the truth lemma, and
every world satisfies symmetry, both units, and type compatibility. Thus a
formula is forced in every lawful Kripke model exactly when it is derivable
from finitely many instances of the full, potentially infinite schema. This
covers finite and countably infinite carriers; no enumeration-free completeness
claim is made for uncountable atom languages.

\paragraph{Enumeration-free completeness.}
The countability condition can be removed by a separate Zorn argument. Consider
the sets of formulas that extend an arbitrary deductively closed theory and
whose finite subcontexts do not derive a chosen forbidden formula. A nonempty
inclusion chain is bounded by its union because every natural-deduction proof
uses only a finite context. A maximal extension therefore exists; maximality
forces deductive closure and disjunction primeness. This yields relative prime
extensions and implication-failure future worlds without a formula schedule,
and hence an enumeration-free canonical truth lemma. Applied to the resonance
law schema, the construction provides a lawful countermodel for every
nonderivable formula and proves strong Kripke completeness for arbitrary
same-universe carriers. The proof is classical and uses choice; no predicative
or choice-free completeness theorem is claimed.

\paragraph{Dependent judgment proof-theoretic semantics.}
The raw dependent $\Pi/\Sigma/\mathsf{Id}$ calculus admits an explicit
inference-rule model interface: formation and typing are arbitrary predicates
closed under every raw constructor. Mutual induction proves soundness in every
such model. The syntactic model interprets validity as nonempty derivability;
it is exact, least among inference-rule models, and stable under well-typed
renaming and substitution. This is a proof-theoretic term-model completeness
theorem, not extensional set-valued or CwF semantics, conversion,
normalization, or higher identity coherence.

\paragraph{Dependent contextual-category comprehension.}
Well-formed raw contexts are objects and extensional well-typed substitutions
are morphisms. Their derivation witnesses are retained propositionally but
erased from morphism equality, yielding strict identity and associativity.
Raw types reindex contravariantly with exact identity and composition laws;
context extension has a display projection and generic variable. A base
substitution and term of the reindexed display type pair uniquely into the
extension, establishing the comprehension universal property. These results
provide the contextual-category comprehension core beneath the existing
set-valued family semantics. A fully bundled CwF with well-formed type and term
presheaves is not claimed.

\paragraph{Dependent type-and-term presheaves.}
Well-formed raw types now form an explicit contravariant reindexing family over
the context category, with strict identity and composition laws. The total
family of typed terms has the same laws, and its projection to the type family
is natural. The precise remaining CwF obstruction is also formalized:
Nat-indexed substitutions into the empty context retain unconstrained irrelevant
components, so its identity and constant-unit endomorphisms are distinct.
Consequently the present empty context is not terminal. A finite-context
extensional quotient or normalization of irrelevant substitution components is
required before claiming a full CwF.

\paragraph{Dependent scoped syntax.}
Mutual depth predicates bound every de Bruijn variable in raw types and terms.
Every lookup position lies below context length, and formation and typing
derivations imply the corresponding scopedness judgments. Hence substitutions
that agree on the finitely many variables of a context act identically on all
well-formed types and well-typed terms.

\paragraph{Finite-support context quotient.}
Quotienting substitutions by this
finite agreement is compatible with composition in both arguments and produces
a strict corrected context category. In that quotient every map into the empty
context is equal, so the empty context is terminal and the preceding obstruction
is discharged. Descending the type/term presheaves and comprehension to this
quotient remains the boundary before a full CwF package.

\paragraph{Finite-support type and typed-term presheaves.}
Well-formed types and total typed terms descend to substitution classes without
choosing representatives: scoped substitution congruence proves both
reindexing operations well defined. Their identity and contravariant
composition laws hold strictly, and the typed-term projection to types is
natural.

\paragraph{Finite-support comprehension universality.}
Context extension, display projection, and the generic variable also descend.
A substitution and term of the reindexed type pair into the extension; both
component equations hold, and the pairing is unique. Thus terminal context,
type and term reindexing, and comprehension universality coexist in the same
corrected category. A conventional dependent term-fiber bundle remains
packaging work before exposing a standard bundled-CwF interface.

\paragraph{Bundled finite syntactic category with families.}
Terms are finally exposed as dependent fibers of the total typed-term space
over each well-formed type. These fibers reindex along quotient substitutions;
identity and composition are expressed by heterogeneous equality across the
propositionally equal fibers. The generic variable inhabits the exact pullback
of the display type, and fiber-level pairing satisfies projection, genericity,
and uniqueness. A single certificate therefore combines terminal context,
type and dependent-term substitution, and split comprehension. This closes the
syntactic CwF construction; comparison with the existing set-valued semantics
remains a separate morphism theorem.

\paragraph{Completed-fragment CwF semantic comparison.}
Every fully coherent completed typing judgment embeds conservatively into the
finite syntactic CwF: its raw context, type, and term are unchanged. The same
certificate supplies the existing set-valued semantic context, type, and term,
and the interpretation commutes with semantic identity substitution through
the certified fiber equivalence. Semantic completion is stated as an explicit
gate on raw CwF terms. We do not claim a morphism on the whole raw CwF;
arbitrary-substitution and comprehension naturality and completion of every raw
judgment remain separate obligations.

\paragraph{Completed-fragment substitution naturality.}
A semantic realization of a raw substitution packages source and target
context interpretations, the semantic substitution, and interpretations of
all replacement variables. It induces the corresponding finite-support
syntactic substitution, and reindexing the embedded term is exactly raw
substitution. The general mutual dispatcher proves arbitrary-substitution
naturality: interpretation of the substituted term agrees with semantic
substitution of the original interpretation. Global realization coverage and
comprehension naturality remain explicit gates before a whole-CwF morphism can
be claimed.

\paragraph{Completed-fragment comprehension naturality.}
Each completed display type selects corresponding finite-syntactic and
set-valued context extensions. Given a semantically realized substitution, the
same completed term determines comprehension pairings on both sides. Projection
and generic-variable equations hold, and the semantic generic component equals
the fiber-transported interpretation of the syntactically substituted term.
Hence comprehension is preserved on the completed realized fragment. Global
judgment completion and semantic-substitution coverage remain the exact gates
before these comparisons assemble into a whole-CwF morphism.

\paragraph{Whole-CwF morphism readiness boundary.}
Coherent telescope synthesis canonically realizes identity substitution for
every completed judgment, including all semantic lookup replacements. The
local judgment, arbitrary-substitution, and comprehension comparison laws are
then bundled with an exact totality boundary: a whole-CwF morphism requires
precisely completion of every finite-CwF term and semantic realization of every
raw substitution into a completed judgment. Neither global coverage statement
is claimed here.

\paragraph{Completed display-projection semantics.}
Every completed display type canonically realizes the nonidentity raw
successor-variable projection. Its semantic map is exactly the set-valued Sigma
projection, every replacement is the corresponding semantic lookup weakened
along that projection, and the induced finite-support morphism is exactly the
syntactic CwF display projection. Nonidentity coverage therefore includes the
fundamental comprehension projection; arbitrary substitutions and global
judgment completion remain open.

\paragraph{Semantic-substitution composition and finite functoriality.}
Well-typed raw substitutions compose by term substitution. Their semantic
assignment maps compose as functions, while replacement interpretations compose
by reindexing the later replacements along the earlier map. A realization into
a fixed interpreted target context is packaged independently of any selected
target term, so every completed realization is closed under precomposition by
such a realization into its source. The induced finite-support morphism is
exactly categorical composition in the syntactic context category. This is a
closure theorem rather than a construction of realizations for all raw
substitutions; arbitrary substitution coverage and global judgment completion
remain open.

\paragraph{Completed-term semantic pairing realization.}
A realized base substitution and completed target term canonically determine a
raw comprehension pairing into the extended context. Its semantic assignment
map is the set-valued Sigma pairing, its newest replacement is the interpreted
substituted term, and all older replacements are inherited from the base
realization. The induced finite-support morphism is exactly the syntactic CwF
pairing. This covers pairings generated by completed target terms; an arbitrary
source-side head component need not be such a reindexing, so global substitution
coverage is not claimed.

\paragraph{General source-side semantic pairing realization.}
The target-term restriction is removed by accepting the exact local semantic
data for an arbitrary well-typed source head: a coherent target formation, its
substitution fiber equivalence, and a source semantic term. Transporting that
term across the equivalence extends any target-independent realized base
substitution to the context extension. The semantic projection and generic
equations hold, and the induced finite-support morphism is exactly the
quotient-CwF pairing. Hence pairing shape is no longer an obstruction; global
coverage is reduced to supplying the explicit formation and typing semantic
data uniformly.

\paragraph{Canonical raw-substitution pairing decomposition.}
Every substitution into an extended target has a canonical tail and a newest
component typed in the display family reindexed along that tail. Re-pairing
recovers its term components exactly, its proof-erased extensional substitution
exactly, and its finite-support class exactly as the CwF comprehension pairing.
Raw substitutions retain proof-relevant typing derivations, so equality of the
proof-relevant structures before erasure is not asserted. Together with the
general semantic pairing theorem, this reduces global semantic-substitution
coverage recursively to uniform formation and typing semantic data for each
head component.

\paragraph{Recursive semantic-substitution realization induction.}
Semantic realization transports across changes of proof-relevant typing
witnesses when the substitution term function is unchanged. Every substitution
into the empty target has a canonical realization, while the extension step
takes a realized canonical tail and the general formation/typing semantics of
the actual source head and realizes the original substitution. This gives an
exact recursive induction principle for global coverage. Uniform synthesis of
the per-head semantic data remains the outstanding premise. The current basic
realization record packages maps and replacement terms; a future strengthened
interface should additionally internalize their lookup-by-lookup coherence,
which the concrete constructors establish through their individual equations.

\paragraph{Intrinsic lookup-coherent semantic substitutions.}
The strengthened realization interface retains a target semantic tree and, for
every target lookup, a fiber equivalence between the selected replacement
family and the target lookup family reindexed by the semantic assignment map.
It also requires equality between the transported replacement term and the
reindexed target variable. Identity substitutions and completed display
projections inhabit this interface with exact target-tree preservation.
Strengthened pairing and composition closure remain before the recursive
coverage induction can be promoted wholesale to this intrinsic interface.

\paragraph{Lookup-coherent closure.}
The \emph{Lookup-coherent pairing and composition closure} theorem shows that
target-independent realizations compose generically. Intrinsic lookup
coherence is preserved under composition by reindexing each later fiber
equivalence along the earlier semantic map and using transport naturality for
the term equation. General source-side pairing is coherent at the newest
lookup by its formation equivalence and at every older lookup by inherited base
coherence. The semantic maps remain exactly function composition and Sigma
pairing. Uniform synthesis of formation and typing semantics for every head is
now the remaining coverage premise for substitutions.

\paragraph{Strengthened coherent whole-CwF readiness boundary.}
Fixing a completion model anchors every target to its canonically synthesized
semantic context and tree. The strengthened global substitution gate requires
intrinsic lookup coherence rather than only an assignment map and replacement
terms. Forgetting coherence recovers the previous completed-substitution and
whole-CwF readiness gates, while strengthened readiness is equivalent exactly
to judgment completion conjoined with canonical coherent substitution
coverage. Neither global gate is claimed; uniform judgment completion and
per-head semantic synthesis remain the totality obligations.

\paragraph{Recursive lookup-coherent substitution induction.}
Transport across proof-relevant substitution witnesses with the same term
function preserves both the semantic assignment map and the exact target tree.
Empty targets have canonical coherent realizations, and a coherent canonical
tail plus general semantic data for the actual source head realizes the
original substitution with every lookup equation intact. The recursive
coverage mechanism therefore lands directly in the strengthened interface;
uniform formation and typing semantic synthesis for every head is its sole
remaining substitution premise.
